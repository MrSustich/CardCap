Common questions

How does the cap work?

When you start a show, pick the most you want to spend. Every card you buy counts down from your cap, and every card you sell adds back to it. You can raise or lower your cap any time during the show with Edit cap.

What happens when a card would take me over?

If the Over-the-cap warning is on, CardCap asks before saving a purchase that brings you to $0 or past your cap. You can buy it anyway, raise your cap, or skip the card. Turn the warning on or off in Settings.

Can I track a show without a budget?

Yes. Turn on No budget mode when you start a show (or choose Always use no budget in Settings). CardCap will track what you spend and what comes in, without a cap.

How do I group cards from one vendor?

On the purchase screen, tap Add more after each card. The vendor and condition stay filled in, and the cards are saved together as one bundle.

Will my data come back on a new iPhone?

Yes, if Sync with iCloud is on in Settings (it's on by default). Sign in with the same Apple ID on your new iPhone, install CardCap, and your shows, cards, photos, and wishlist come back automatically. Syncing can take a few minutes, and photos may take a little longer.

Can I keep my own backup?

Yes. In Settings, tap Export a backup file and save it wherever you like, such as the Files app. Restore from a backup file adds anything missing and never replaces what's already in the app.

How do I find a card I bought?

Open My cards, tap the magnifying glass, and type a card name, vendor, show, or grade. You can also filter by Raw, Graded, Sealed, or Trades.

How do I delete my data?

In Settings, tap Delete all CardCap data. This removes your shows, cards, photos, and wishlist from this iPhone and, if iCloud sync is on, from your iCloud too.
